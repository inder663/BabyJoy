Overview

Welcome to BabyJoy, a lifestyle application designed to help parents log and track their newborn's daily activities, including feeding, sleep, and diaper changes. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your information while using our app.

  • BabyJoy does not collect any personal data from users.
  • We do not use analytics tools to gather information about user behavior.
  • In-app purchases do not require us to collect personal data.

Information We Collect

Our app is designed with user privacy in mind and does not collect any personal information. We do not ask for or store names, email addresses, or any demographic data.

How We Use Data

Since we do not collect any user data, we do not use data in any capacity. BabyJoy is purely a tool for users to manage their newborn's daily logs.

Sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties because we do not collect any data in the first place.

Analytics

BabyJoy does not utilize any analytics services to track user data or app usage.

Cookies/Tracking

We do not employ cookies or any tracking mechanisms, as we do not collect any personal data from our users.

Data Retention

As we do not collect or store personal information, there is nothing to retain or delete.

Security

While we take reasonable steps to protect user data, please remember that our app does not collect personal information. Therefore, there are no data breaches to mitigate.

Children's Privacy

BabyJoy is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of any personal data collected from a child, we will take steps to delete such data immediately.

International Users

This Privacy Policy is intended to comply with international data protection laws. As we do not collect user data, we are not subject to data transfer regulations.

Your Rights

As we do not collect personal data, you have no data rights in relation to the information we do not hold.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future. Any changes will be communicated through the app or our website. Please review the Privacy Policy regularly for updates.
